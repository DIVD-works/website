Safety

Creating a respectful and secure environment for learning and work.

People first

DIVD.Works aims to make our programs and projects safe, respectful, and inclusive for students, schools, businesses, and partners.

Working safely

  • Treat other participants and partners with respect.
  • Follow the rules and security requirements of each project or workplace.
  • Do not access, use, or share systems and information without permission.
  • Report concerns, incidents, or unsafe behavior as soon as possible.

Report a concern

If you believe someone may be at risk or a security issue needs attention, contact safety@divd.works. For immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency services first.
