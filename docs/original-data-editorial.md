# Original-data editorial series

The platform-data idea is approved as a direction, not as permission to expose
application or project records. Before the first data-led article, the
editorial owner must approve:

- the privacy-safe aggregate dataset;
- the minimum cell-size and suppression rules;
- the recurring metric definitions;
- the methodology and date range;
- a reviewer for confidentiality and accuracy.

Until those inputs exist, the website publishes the methodology and practical
guidance but does **not** publish invented counts, conversion rates, placement
rates, or skills-demand claims. The current public opportunity records are
operational content, not a validated longitudinal dataset.
