# DIVD.Works first-party content strategy

**Status:** implementation baseline, 21 September 2026  
**Owner:** DIVD.Works editorial owner — assignment still required  
**Review rule:** every published page needs a named reviewer before production release.

## Content pillars

1. **Student pathways** — internship, graduation project, real-world project, and early-career decisions.
2. **Employer practice** — scoping assignments, supervising junior technical talent, and turning a good project into a hiring pipeline.
3. **School practice** — placement readiness, school–employer collaboration, and safe progression into real work.
4. **Technical trust** — safe project boundaries, privacy, responsible disclosure, and clear expectations.
5. **People and progress** — source-backed stories about the people and work behind DIVD.Works.
6. **Platform insights** — only privacy-safe, aggregated observations with a published methodology.

## Evidence and authorship standard

- Use first-party pages, approved announcements, or a named subject-matter reviewer as the source.
- Label guidance, experience, opinion, and measured outcome separately.
- Never publish a number, success rate, scarcity claim, or employer/student outcome without a source and date.
- Do not expose personal, confidential, or identifiable application/project data.
- Every page has one audience, one next action, and at least one reviewable source link. The resource pages in this release use current first-party guidance and public pathway pages as context; a named editorial owner/reviewer is still required before production publication.

## Publication model

- Draft: author writes from the brief and records sources.
- Review: an editorial owner checks accuracy, safety, and claims.
- Release: the page is generated into the static first-party newsroom/resources surface.
- Review cadence: monthly inventory check; quarterly pillar and conversion review.
