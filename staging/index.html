OUR VISION

Talent isn’t the problem.
The path to using it is.

.Works is the architect connecting motivated young talent with the professional IT world.

We bridge the gap between education and the IT industry. Students apply for internships, and companies submit IT projects for development. Qualified interns then apply directly to these projects, which can range from custom website builds all the way to comprehensive pentesting, creating a bridge between learning and doing.

This supports both the students' growth and the professional's goals: businesses get talent trained for the task, while students gain high-value, real-world experience.

ONE PLATFORM, THREE ROLES

Built for students,
businesses, and schools

Students

Apply for internships and real IT projects — from website builds to pentesting. Get coaching, mentoring and career development alongside your first job.

Businesses

Submit IT projects for development and get talent trained specifically for your task — pre-vetted, motivated, and supported by our program along the way.

Schools

Keep students enrolled and on track for their diploma with guaranteed internship access, reducing dropouts caused by the internship shortage in Dutch IT.
