Accessibility

We want DIVD.Works to be usable by as many people as possible.

Our commitment

We are working to make this website accessible and easy to use, including for people who use assistive technology or alternative ways to navigate the web.

What we are working toward

Our goal is to align the website with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA. We review accessibility as we improve the site, but some content or interactions may still need attention.

  • Keyboard-friendly navigation and visible focus states.
  • Clear headings, meaningful link text, and semantic page structure.
  • Readable text, sufficient color contrast, and responsive layouts.
  • Alternative text for meaningful images and labels for interactive controls.
  • Respect for reduced-motion preferences where animations are used.

Feedback and support

If you have trouble using any part of the website, please let us know what you were trying to do and which page was involved. Email accessibility@divd.works and we will use your feedback to improve the experience.
