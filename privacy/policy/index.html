Privacy policy

How we handle your data and protect your privacy.

Effective date: 21 August 2026

1. Introduction

DIVD.Works (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you visit our website, use our services, or interact with us.

By using our website, you agree to the practices described in this Policy.

2. Information we collect

We may collect information that identifies you or can be used to contact you, including:

  • Name, email address, and contact details you provide when you register, apply, or contact us.
  • Information about your role, organization, and project interests.
  • Website usage data such as browser type, device information, pages visited, and referring URLs.
  • Technical information such as IP address, timestamps, and system logs.

We also collect information you voluntarily submit through forms, applications, or communications with us.

3. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Provide and improve our services, platform, and website.
  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests.
  • Process applications, memberships, or partnerships.
  • Communicate updates, opportunities, or administrative information.
  • Monitor platform performance, maintain security, and prevent misuse.
  • Comply with legal, contractual, and regulatory obligations.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with:

  • Trusted service providers that help us operate the website or provide technical support.
  • Partners or organizations when required to fulfill a request you have made.
  • Authorities or legal representatives when required by law or to protect our rights.

Where appropriate, we require third parties to handle personal data in a manner consistent with this Policy and applicable laws.

5. Cookies and analytics

We may use cookies, tracking pixels, and similar technologies to understand how visitors use our website and improve the experience. These tools may collect information such as page visits, referrer information, and general device information.

You can configure your browser to reject or delete cookies, although some features of the website may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

6. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

7. Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection.

8. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of inaccurate or unnecessary data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Request data portability where applicable.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.

9. Third-party links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external websites, and we encourage you to read their privacy policies before sharing personal information.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your personal data, please contact us at:

Email: privacy@divd.works

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page regularly.
