Welcoming Our New COO & Chief Creative Officer: Nigel van Maanen

By DIVD.works · Published 17 April 2025

We proudly welcome Nigel van Maanen to DIVD.works as our new Chief Creative Officer (CCO). In his role, Nigel will serve as the guardian of our creative and organizational vision, helping shape the heart of everything we do.

Nigel’s mission is to ensure that all of our projects—whether public-facing initiatives, internal tools, or collaborative outreach—reflect our values: ethical innovation, civic responsibility, and digital safety. His role blends creative leadership with strategic oversight, guiding our teams from concept to execution with purpose and clarity.
