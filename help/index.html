Frequently asked questions

Who can join DIVD.Works?

DIVD.Works is for students, businesses, and schools. Students can apply for internships and IT projects, businesses can submit real work, and schools can connect students to structured learning opportunities.

Is it free for students?

Yes, students can access the platform and opportunities without paying. Some premium support tracks are available depending on the level of coaching or program support needed.

How do I apply for an internship or project?

Visit the Jobs section, browse the current opportunities, and apply through the relevant listing. If you are unsure which role fits best, you can also contact us for guidance.

Can businesses submit their own projects?

Yes. Businesses can post real IT assignments and challenges. We help match them with student talent and support the process so the work is useful for both sides.

What kind of work is suitable?

We work with practical IT tasks such as website builds, automation, technical research, testing, and cybersecurity-related assignments. The goal is to make the work useful for both the business and the learner.

How do schools participate?

Schools can support students by helping them find internships and project placements that align with their education and graduation requirements. This gives students a clearer route from classroom learning to professional experience.
